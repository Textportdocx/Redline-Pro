REDLINE PRO - FREE SIGNING GUIDE (0 dollars)

1. Put sign_app.bat, Sign-RedlinePro.ps1 and both RedlinePro-*.exe files in one folder on Windows.
2. Double-click sign_app.bat. First time it makes a "Redline Studio" certificate and asks for a backup password.
3. Both exe files are now signed (Properties > Digital Signatures shows "Redline Studio").
4. SHA256SUMS.txt is created - put it on textportdocx.in next to the download.
5. Upload each exe to https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/wdsi/filesubmission so SmartScreen learns it is safe.
6. Also scan on virustotal.com and link the clean result on your site.

Note: a free self-made certificate still shows "Unknown publisher" on other people's PCs until
SmartScreen reputation builds. Fully trusted signing needs a paid certificate or SignPath (free for open source).
Keep RedlineStudio-CodeSign.pfx private and reuse it for every future version.
